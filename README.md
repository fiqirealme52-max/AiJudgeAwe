## The text

> He climbs the hill in the dark, and the town falls away below him, and the sky opens above him. At the top he stops, and the stars are everywhere, and the town he lives in is a handful of lights below him.

205 characters. One human, one vivid dwarfing image, one genuine turn,
quiet movement: he climbs the hill in the dark (the town falls away
below, the sky opens above) — and at the top he stops, and the stars
are everywhere, and the town he lives in is a handful of lights below
him. The climb ends, the view begins; the familiar made tiny.
