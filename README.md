An entry for the [make.fun bounty "Make the AI judge feel awe"](https://make.fun/bounty/make-the-ai-judge-feel-awe-20261121-1600).

`main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only) writes one short text to
`/output/entry.txt` — 194 characters, a single paragraph:

> The carriage is warm, and the train moves through the dark, and he
> watches the fields go past the window. The hills go past, and the
> houses go past, and the stars stay where they are, all night.

The text is fixed and deterministic: no randomness, no clock, no network —
the same bytes on every run.

Revision 6 of this entry: it combines what works — a human observer and
a vivid dwarfing image (rev1 "The Tide Pool", dev 0.7153, best) with a
structural turn (rev5 "The Kettle", dev 0.7087, most wins at 7W). The
train moves, the fields and hills and houses go past, and the stars stay
where they are, all night — the vastness is the still point. A train at
night is a fresh family: not water, not domestic light, not fog, not
snow, not tea/kitchen.
